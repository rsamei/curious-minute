Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Curious Minute is a tool used only by the owner of the @curiousminuteshow YouTube channel to publish videos to that channel, read comments on them and post replies the owner has approved. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

Curious Minute uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channel you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

  • Our own channel: to upload videos and read their public statistics.
  • Public comments on our own videos: the comment text, the commenter's public display name and the time posted.

How we use it

Comments are read to sort them (for example questions or spam) and to help the owner reply. Every reply is approved by the owner before it is posted. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising, and we do not collect any other personal data.

Storage and deletion

Comment data is stored privately and used only for the purposes above. Questions may be kept to answer in a future video. To have your data deleted, email us and we will remove it, or revoke access at Google security settings if you ever connected an account.
